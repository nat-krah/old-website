Mini Projects

Snake in Terminal

As part of an effort to learn more c++ and Unix Terminals, I decided to code the game snake from the ground up. I used limited resources, only looked at ansi escape codes, terminal flags, and c++ documentation. Basic code was ported over to JavaScript and can be played here.

Arch Linux

I recently switched to a different laptop, and decided that it would be a good opportunity to switch operating systems. I decided to switch to Arch Linux because its incredible customization would grant me a greater understanding of the inner workings of operating systems, reduce bloatware, streamline my workflow, and give me an opportunity to learn unix cli syntax.

Final project for MECE 104, engineering design tools. Tasked with designing a machine to play a selected song with chimes using limited resources. Sandy the Beach Cleanup Robot

A challenge issued by Onshape challenged applicants to use vex materials to design a robot that would help the environment. My robotics team and I decided to tackle ocean trash with a beach cleanup robot. It would sift large sections of the sand and deposit any trash into a removable bin. I worked on the sifter and trash bin.
