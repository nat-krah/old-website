Projects

SPEX CubeSat

In Cubesat we are exploring different radiation deflection/diversion methods in the hope of finding a lighter and cheaper way to protect electric components and astronauts. We are working on a small 1U cubesat with several radiation sensors to get baseline data and prove mission success before expanding to a full research satellite. Additionally, we are constructing several High Altitude Balloons (HABs) for individual component testing and practice assembly. I am working as Payload Lead organizing efforts to design radiation detectors to integrate with the larger 1U cubesat.

Photo: Image from SPEX High Altitude Balloon

All Seeing Eye

The "All Seeing Eye" is a robotic tentacle that moves in complex patterns and will use machine vision to track peoples faces. While working on the Mechanical team we redesigned and rebuilt the entire arm and power system to reduce strain on the stepper motors and core. This coming semester I will be working as co-lead and hope to finish the rebuild and integrate machine vision.

Vex Over Under

Robot designed and built to compete in VEX's Over Under Challenge. I was co-lead and worked on CAD Model, Building, Coding, Notebook and was primary driver at competition. We reached the semi finals in the State Competition. The first iteration featured a four motor PTO, catapult, intake, sleds, and pneumatic “wings”. The second iteration featured a band assisted elevation mechanism, a raised puncher, a pneumatic blocker, an intake, sleds, and wings.

Floating Wind Turbine

With the increasing threat of global warming, now, more than ever, it is vital that we work to build clean, reliable energy sources. UMaine has organized efforts to implore students to create new methods of supporting wind turbines for further development in the gulf of Maine. Our design features a long cylindrical base with a large weight on the bottom.

Photo: Wind turbine competing at UMaine's Windstorm Challenge

VEX Spin Up

Robot designed to compete in VEX's Spin Up Challenge. First iteration featured ratcheting 3000 rpm 4” flywheel, top loading hopper, and intake. Second iteration featured 3600 rpm 3” two stage flywheel, bottom loading hopper, intake, roller, string launcher, sleds, and custom odometry for position tracking. I specialised in the intake and hopper system and string launcher.
