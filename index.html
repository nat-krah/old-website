About

Hi!
I'm Nat, a second year at RIT studing mechanical, aerospace, and computer engineering. For as long as I can remember I've loved putting things together. From simple legos in Elementery school to speocalizing in designing complex parts for a non-uniform robotic arm. My life has been a series of projects, and this website is an attempt to document there highlights.
